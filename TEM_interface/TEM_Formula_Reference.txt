========== GUIDE LEGEND ==========

Cell Color in Excel → Meaning:
  Blue   = Links to another tab (cross-sheet reference)
  Red    = Hard Input (admin-editable value)
  Black  = Calculation using links from current tab
  Pink   = Links to external file
  Green  = Calculation using Named Values / Arrays
  Grey   = Not required / No data

Tab Color → Sheet Category:
  Yellow = Title, Revision History, Disclaimer, Guide
  Purple = Charts (output dashboards)
  Red    = Summary sheets
  Green  = Inputs / Quotes (Admin-editable)
  Blue   = Calculation sheets (do NOT manually type)

Mining Operation Switch (Assumptions_Dashboard!L4):
  "Departmental" = Owner operates all mining (no MDO)
  "MDO"          = Mine Developer Operator model (outsourced)

Pre-Tax / Pre-Finance Switch (Assumptions_Dashboard!L6):
  "Yes" = Pre-Tax, Pre-Finance basis
  "No"  = Full model with debt and tax

  ='Owner CAPEX'!D41 + 'MDO CAPEX'!D34 = D41

  Assumptions_Dashboard!L4   = Mining Operation type ("Departmental" / "MDO")

SECTION 7 – GOVERNMENT FEES & TAXES — Government Sheet
================================================================================

Sheet: Government
Purpose: Calculates all statutory government fees, taxes, and charges year-by-year

---- Header/Production Data ----

E2:   ='Production Schedule'!E1   [Year label]
E3:   ='Production Schedule'!E3   [Project year number]

E6:   =IF(Assumptions_Dashboard!$L$8="NCI", 'Coal Price'!E7*(1+Sensitivity!$C$6), 0)
      [NCI price if price type = NCI, with sensitivity factor]
E7:   =IF(Assumptions_Dashboard!$L$8="Commercial", 'Coal Price'!E8*(1+Sensitivity!$C$6), 0)
      [Commercial price if price type = Commercial]

Revenue:
E8:   =E6*E4/10    [Revenue at NCI price (INR Cr)]
E9:   =E7*E4/10    [Revenue at Commercial price (INR Cr)]

---- Government Fees Calculations ----

B12: Revenue Sharing
  D12 = =Assumptions_Dashboard!$D$120    [Bid premium rate = 21%]
  E12 = ='Coal Price'!E7 * Government!E4 * $D$12 / 10
  [= Commercial price × coal production × revenue share rate / 10 (Cr conversion)]
  Key input: Assumptions_Dashboard!D120 = 0.21 (Bid Premium / Revenue Sharing %)
             Assumptions_Dashboard!D121 = "Commercial Sale Price" (basis for revenue sharing)

B13: Upfront Payment Paid (per year)
  E13 = =Assumptions_Dashboard!$D$133 * 25%
  [Upfront Amount × 25% installment; total = Assumptions_Dashboard!D133 = 99.07 Cr]

B14: Upfront Cumulative Paid (eligible for refund)
  [Running sum of E13 over project life]

B16: Upfront payment that can be adjusted (eligible for refund)
  D16 = =SUM('Project CAPEX'!E12:AR12)   [Total upfront amounts paid during construction]

B17: Adjusted Revenue Sharing
  E17 = =E12 - E16
  [Revenue Sharing less any upfront payment eligible for offset]

B18: Final Revenue Sharing (after adjustment)
  E18 = =E17

B19: GST on Revenue Sharing
  D19 = =Assumptions_Dashboard!D126      [GST on Revenue Sharing rate = 18%]
  E19 = =E18 * $D$19
  [Final Revenue Sharing × 18% GST]

B20: Royalty
  D20 = =Assumptions_Dashboard!D122      [Royalty rate = 14% of CIL notified price]
  E20 = ='Coal Price'!E7 * Government!E4 * $D$20 / 10
  [Commercial price × production × 14% / 10]

B21: District Mineral Foundation (DMF)
  D21 = =Assumptions_Dashboard!D123      [DMF rate = 10% of Royalty]
  E21 = =E20 * $D$21
  [Royalty × 10%]

B22: National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET)
  D22 = =Assumptions_Dashboard!D124      [NMET rate = 3% of Royalty]
  E22 = =E20 * $D$22
  [Royalty × 3%]

B23: Compensation Cess
  D23 = =Assumptions_Dashboard!$D$130   [= "Omitted" / zero in current model]
  E23 = =D23 * E4 / 10

B24: Surface Rent
  D24 = =(Assumptions_Dashboard!$D$134 * Assumptions_Dashboard!$D$135) / 10^7
  [Surface Rent Rate (INR/ha) × ML Area (ha) / 10^7 = annual surface rent in INR Cr]
  Key inputs:
    Assumptions_Dashboard!D134 = 2,000 INR/ha (Surface Rent rate; Hard Input)
    Assumptions_Dashboard!D135 = =D13 = 1,048 ha (ML Area = Block Area)

B25: Vayapar Kar    [OMITTED in current model]
B26: Public Works and Road Cess  [OMITTED in current model]
Rows 27–29: Space for additional future fees

B30: GST on Royalty, DMF, NMET, etc.
  D30 = =Assumptions_Dashboard!D127      [GST rate = 18%]
  E30 = =SUM(E20:E29) * $D$30
  [Sum of Royalty + DMF + NMET + Cess + Surface Rent × 18% GST]

B31: Total Government Fees (excl. Mine Closure)
  E31 = =SUM(E18:E30)
  [Sum of Final Revenue Sharing + GST on Rev Share + Royalty + DMF + NMET
   + Cess + Surface Rent + GST on Royalty]
